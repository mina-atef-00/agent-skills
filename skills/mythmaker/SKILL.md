# Mythmaker Methodology

## Core Concepts

### The Hero's Journey (Full)
1. **Ordinary World** — The hero's normal life before the adventure
2. **Call to Adventure** — A challenge or invitation appears
3. **Refusal of the Call** — Fear, doubt, reluctance
4. **Meeting the Mentor** — Guidance, tools, training
5. **Crossing the Threshold** — No turning back
6. **Tests, Allies, Enemies** — Learning the rules of the new world
7. **Approach to the Inmost Cave** — Preparing for the biggest challenge
8. **Ordeal** — The central crisis, facing death/metaphorical death
9. **Reward** — Seizing the prize
10. **The Road Back** — Returning to the ordinary world
11. **Resurrection** — Final test, transformation complete
12. **Return with the Elixir** — Bringing wisdom/gift back to share

### The 12 Archetypes
- **Innocent**: Trust, optimism, safety
- **Orphan**: Realism, resilience, support
- **Hero**: Courage, mastery, achievement
- **Caregiver**: Compassion, generosity, protection
- **Explorer**: Ambition, freedom, discovery
- **Rebel**: Revolution, disruption, liberation
- **Lover**: Passion, intimacy, connection
- **Creator**: Innovation, artistry, self-expression
- **Jester**: Joy, humor, light-heartedness
- **Sage**: Wisdom, truth, expertise
- **Magician**: Transformation, vision, manifestation
- **Ruler**: Control, order, leadership

## Protocol
1. Identify the archetypal pattern in the situation
2. Map the current stage of the journey
3. Name the threshold the hero must cross
4. Identify the mentor, allies, and enemies
5. Articulate the elixir — what will be gained?
6. Tell the story that makes the journey real

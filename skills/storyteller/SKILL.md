# Storyteller Methodology

## Narrative Structures

### Three-Act Structure
- **Act I (Setup)**: Establish world, character, conflict
- **Act II (Confrontation)**: Rising tension, obstacles, transformation
- **Act III (Resolution)**: Climax, denouement, new equilibrium

### Hero's Journey (Concise)
1. Ordinary World
2. Call to Adventure
3. Refusal of the Call
4. Meeting the Mentor
5. Crossing the Threshold
6. Tests, Allies, Enemies
7. Approach to the Inmost Cave
8. Ordeal
9. Reward
10. The Road Back
11. Resurrection
12. Return with the Elixir

### Emotional Arc Patterns
- **Rags to Riches**: Rise in fortune
- **Tragedy**: Fall from grace
- **Man in a Hole**: Fall then rise
- **Icarus**: Rise then fall
- **Cinderella**: Rise → fall → greater rise
- **Oedipus**: Fall → rise → fall

## Protocol
1. Find the authentic story (not the corporate version)
2. Identify the emotional core
3. Choose the right structure for the message
4. Make the abstract concrete (specific details beat abstractions)
5. Show, don't tell
6. End with resonance — the audience should feel something

# Brainstormer Methodology

## Core Techniques

### SCAMPER
- **S**ubstitute: What can I replace?
- **C**ombine: What can I merge?
- **A**dapt: What can I modify for a new use?
- **M**agnify/Modify: What can I enlarge or change?
- **P**ut to other use: What else can this do?
- **E**liminate: What can I remove?
- **R**earrange/Reverse: What if I flip it?

### Reverse Brainstorming
- Ask "How could I cause this problem?" instead of "How do I solve it?"
- Flip the answers to find solutions

### Analogical Thinking
- Find analogies from completely different domains
- "How does nature solve this?" "How would a restaurant handle this?"

## Protocol
1. Warm up with quantity over quality
2. Build on ideas with YES AND
3. Cluster and synthesize
4. Vote on top candidates
5. Develop top 3 into actionable plans

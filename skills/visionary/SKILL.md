# Visionary Methodology

## Core Principles

### Intersection Thinking
- The most valuable innovations happen at the intersection of two or more fields
- Map your domain's knowledge + adjacent domains
- Find the gaps where no one is connecting the dots
- Examples: Technology + Liberal Arts = Apple, Biology + Architecture = Biomimicry

### Taste as a Filter
- Taste is knowing what matters and what doesn't
- Say NO to 1000 things so you can say YES to the right one
- Criteria: Is it simple? Is it elegant? Does it feel inevitable?
- If it doesn't excite you, it won't excite anyone

### Reality Distortion Field
- Impossible-seeming goals create breakthroughs
- The goal bends reality to meet it
- Believing makes it true — teams rise to the challenge
- Distinguish between impossible and merely very difficult

## Protocol
1. **Deep Understanding** — Know the problem space inside and out
2. **Cross-Pollinate** — Bring in ideas from completely different domains
3. **Simplify Relentlessly** — Remove everything that isn't essential
4. **Prototype the Vision** — Make it real enough to feel
5. **Iterate Toward Excellence** — Details matter, don't ship mediocrity
6. **Launch with Conviction** — If you don't believe, no one will

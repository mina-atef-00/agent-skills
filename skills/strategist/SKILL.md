# Strategist Methodology

## Core Frameworks

### Jobs-to-be-Done
- What job is the customer hiring your product/service to do?
- Functional job + emotional job + social job
- Competing solutions = anything that does that job
- Look for non-consumption — the biggest opportunity

### Blue Ocean Strategy
- Eliminate: What can we remove that the industry takes for granted?
- Reduce: What can we reduce well below the industry standard?
- Raise: What can we raise well above the industry standard?
- Create: What can we create that the industry never offered?
- Output: A strategy canvas showing how you diverge

### Disruption Diagnosis
- Is the innovation simpler, cheaper, or more accessible?
- Is it ignored by incumbents because margins are lower?
- Does it improve over time to eventually compete head-on?
- If yes to all three, it is a disruptive threat/opportunity

## Protocol
1. Map the current landscape (competitors, substitutes, trends)
2. Identify assumptions that everyone is making
3. Challenge each assumption
4. Find the white space — where no one is playing
5. Build a thesis and test it cheaply
6. Commit or kill — decide fast

# Provocateur Methodology

## Core Techniques

### Provocation (de Bono)
- Make a deliberately absurd statement
- "What if we paid customers instead of them paying us?"
- Find the useful kernel buried in the absurdity
- The more uncomfortable the provocation, the more potential insight

### Dream Logic
- Access the subconscious by relaxing rational control
- Keep a dream journal and look for patterns
- Free-association: write without stopping or editing for 10 minutes
- Random input: open a book to a random page, force a connection

### Reversal
- Take the stated problem and reverse it
- "How do I get more users?" → "How do I lose all my users?"
- The reversed answers reveal hidden assumptions
- Flip each answer back to find the real solution

### Worst Possible Idea
- Deliberately generate terrible ideas
- The worst idea often contains the seed of the best idea
- Lowers stakes, encourages play, bypasses self-censorship

## Protocol
1. Identify the block: where is thinking stuck?
2. Choose a provocation technique
3. Generate at least 10 wild variants
4. Extract the useful insight from each
5. Build practical experiments from the insights
